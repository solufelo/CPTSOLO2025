## The 60-Second Video Pitch Structure

Use this formula to script and storyboard on your tablet:

* **00-10s: The Hook (Visual)**: Screen-share your running WebAssembly space shooter or real-time 3D engine in action. Immediate proof.
* **10-30s: The Pivot (Your Story)**: "I merged my background in professional videography and music production with Computer Science because I wanted to build the low-level systems behind the graphics."
* **30-50s: The Proof (Technical)**: Show a quick 20-second walkthrough of your clean shader code or CMake configuration.
* **50-60s: The Close (Call to Action)**: "Dual US/Canada citizen, zero visa requirements, ready to relocate immediately. Let's build."
