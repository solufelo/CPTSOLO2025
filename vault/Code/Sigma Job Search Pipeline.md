# Sigma Job Search Pipeline Plan

Optimized route to land a full-time junior/developer role in Canada or the US using dual citizenship, graphics/C++ skills, and videography.

## S-Tier — Direct Video Pitch & Interactive Demo (The "Aimpill" Strategy)
*Standard job applications have a <1% response rate. You have a videographer background and built a real-time C++ 3D engine. Use them.*

1. **Record a 60-second Pitch**:
   - Screen-share your running C++ WebAssembly space shooter in the browser.
   - Quick code highlight: show your programmable shader pipeline (GLSL) or fixed-timestep game loop.
   - Professional framing and audio (utilizing WLU athletic video gear and Waves/iZotope audio VST chains).
2. **Cold Outreach**:
   - Identify tech leads, engineering managers, or founders at target studios/agencies.
   - DM on LinkedIn or cold email with the 60s pitch link and your compiled [resume.pdf](file:///c:/Users/Administrator/Projects/captainsoloHQ/resume.pdf).
   - *Narrative:* "I don't just write code — I build real-time 3D web engines and ship them."

## A-Tier — Automated Scraping & Rapid Customization (The "JobScanner" Loop)
*Automate discovery and customize resumes for specific job descriptions.*

1. **Discovery via [JobScanner-Pro](file:///C:/Users/Administrator/Projects/JobScanner-Pro)**:
   - Scrape Indeed, JobBank, and Craigslist daily for junior developer, web engineer, and graphics roles.
   - Target both Canada and US remote/hybrid roles (explicitly leveraging your dual citizenship for zero visa requirements).
2. **Dynamic Resume Compiles**:
   - Create two variations of your [resume.tex](file:///c:/Users/Administrator/Projects/captainsoloHQ/resume.tex):
     * *Graphics/Engine Focus* (prioritizing C++, OpenGL, WebAssembly, GLSL).
     * *Full-Stack/Agency Focus* (prioritizing React, Next.js, Node.js, Prisma, SQL).
   - Use your local TinyTeX compiler (`pdflatex`) to generate the tailored PDF in seconds.
