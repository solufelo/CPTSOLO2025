# ADHD Focus Protocol — Eisenhower + Pomodoro

How to manage executive dysfunction, task avoidance, and attention drift using systematic timeboxing and identity anchors.

---

## 1. The Eisenhower + Pomodoro Combo

ADHD brains freeze when a task is too abstract. Link your Eisenhower quadrants directly to specific Pomodoro slots:

* **Quadrant 1 (Do Now — Urgent & Important)**:
  - *The Rule:* Allocate the **first Pomodoro of the work block** to Q1.
  - *Why:* Solve your urgent stressors (like auditing school debt or answering critical emails) when your prefrontal cortex is freshest. Clear the anxiety first so it doesn't leak into your deep work.
* **Quadrant 2 (Schedule — Important, Not Urgent)**:
  - *The Rule:* Use the **50/10 or 25/5 structure** for your Math and Build blocks.
  - *Why:* Break the session into hyper-specific micro-tasks. Never write "Study Math" as a goal. Write: "Solve 3 equations on page 14." Make the goal visual and finite.
* **Quadrant 3 (Delegate / Noise)**:
  - *The Rule:* Batch all Q3 tasks into a single 15-minute block at the end of the day. Do not touch them during deep work.
* **Quadrant 4 (Delete / Slop)**:
  - *The Rule:* Absolutely no screens during your Pomodoro breaks.
  - *Why:* Looking at your phone during a 5-minute break resets your dopamine baseline and makes returning to work feel painful. Walk, stretch, or drink water instead.

## 2. ADHD Hacks for Solomon's Persona

* **The Physical Anchor (Whiteboard / Tablet)**:
  - *How:* Never solve coding logic or math in your head. Write and draw everything on your tablet in Excalidraw. Squeezing a pen engages motor-memory and locks your focus to the canvas.
* **The Camera Mirror (Sony a6100)**:
  - *How:* Turn on your stream setup when studying or building.
  - *Why:* The camera acts as a third-person monitor. It forces self-awareness and immediately halts compulsive alt-tabbing.
* **The Neuro-Shock Baseline**:
  - *How:* Calisthenics followed by a cold shower.
  - *Why:* Spikes norepinephrine and dopamine naturally, providing a clean 2 to 3-hour focus window. Do your hardest math/code immediately after the cold shock.
