# The Full Reading List — Beyond the 14 Weeks

The math arc (../tracks/REDEMPTION-ARC.md) covers Phase -1. This is everything after, in order.
Every book converts something you already DO into something you UNDERSTAND.

## Phase 0 — The Operating System (read alongside everything, from day one)

- **How to Solve It** — Pólya. Understand -> plan -> execute -> look back. The debugging loop, the aim loop, the business loop
- **The Art of Doing Science and Engineering** — Hamming. Critical thinking as a way of life. One chapter a week, like scripture
- **Stories of Your Life and Others** — Ted Chiang. Non-linear time, linguistics, hard science. Narratives that build structural thought

## Phase 1 — Learn to Prove (the true cognitive unlock)

- **Book of Proof** — Hammack (FREE PDF). Sets, logic, induction, contradiction. Start once algebra is comfortable
- **Discrete Mathematics and Its Applications** — Rosen. The CS workhorse; pairs with Laurier discrete courses
- **Gödel, Escher, Bach** — Hofstadter. Formal systems, isomorphism, AI recursion, and discrete logic loops

## Phase 2 — The Two Pillars (order by current obsession)

- **Introduction to Linear Algebra** — Strang (+ free MIT 18.06 lectures). Your graphics/Blender work IS linear algebra
- **3D Math Primer for Graphics and Game Development** — Dunn & Parberry. Bridge from Strang to ProjectPrism/Light Years code
- **Introduction to Probability** — Blitzstein & Hwang (FREE + Stat 110 lectures). The math of your AI tools and of every ranked match

## Phase 3 — Algorithms (finish what Big-O-Exercise started in 2022)

- **The Algorithm Design Manual** — Skiena. Practitioner-first, war stories, better first read than CLRS
- **Algorithms** — Erickson (FREE PDF). The proofs behind Skiena's intuition

## Phase 4 — Systems + Science (the deep end)

- **Code** — Petzold. Relays -> gates -> CPU. Nothing in a computer stays magic
- **Computer Systems: A Programmer's Perspective** — Bryant & O'Hallaron. Memory, cache, linking — why frame times do what they do
- **The Feynman Lectures on Physics, Vol I** (FREE online). Watch the greatest explainer reason from first principles; it transfers to everything

## How to Run It

- One Phase 2+ book at a time, tied to a LIVE project (Strang while writing renderer features, Blitzstein while building AI tools)
- Pen-and-paper work or it doesn't count
- Proof before Algorithms; everything else flexes
