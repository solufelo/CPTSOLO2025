# French Track — Bilingual Revenue Unlock

**S-Tier:** Drill *Vocabulaire Progressif* to hit **2000 word threshold** for **B2 French certification**. Use that cert to bid on **federal government tech contracts** through CaptainSolo.

**Better alternative:** Live language exchanges. Books build vocabulary; conversations build reactivity. Do both: book daily, exchange 2x/week when ready.

## Why This Matters

- Federal gov contracts in Canada often want bilingual capacity
- CaptainSolo becomes a bid vehicle, not just a portfolio shop
- B2 = credible, not tourist French

## Daily Block (20 min, after math)

- Vocabulaire Progressif: one unit, handwritten in Obsidian
- Active recall: cover English, write French, check
- Log new words in `vault/French/Word Bank.md`

## Milestones

| Target | Action |
|---|---|
| 500 words | Baseline self-test |
| 1000 words | Start light conversation practice |
| 2000 words | Register for B2 exam prep |
| B2 certified | Add to CaptainSolo site + LinkedIn |
| Federal bids | Monitor buyandsell.gc.ca, MERX, ProServices |

## Federal Contract Pipeline (post-B2)

1. Register business (BN if not done)
2. PSPC supplier registration
3. Watch for IT consulting / web dev / software RFPs marked bilingual
4. Lead with StudyOS + WASM demo as technical proof, French cert as eligibility unlock

## Weekly

- 1 hour structured study (book)
- Optional: 30 min language exchange (italki, local meetup, Discord FR tech groups)
