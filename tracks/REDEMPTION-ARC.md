# The Redemption Arc — Math & Cognition Rebuild

Goal: from "null" to calculus-ready in ~14 weeks, using books already in the library.
Method: attempt-first, by hand, every day. Relearning is 5-10x faster than first learning — the pathways are dormant, not gone.

## The Library (owned, downloaded)

| Book | Role |
|---|---|
| Humongous Book of Basic Math & Pre-Algebra (Kelley) | Act I spine |
| Master Essential Algebra Skills (McMullen) | Daily drill book — the "Aimlabs of math" |
| Algebra Essentials Practice Workbook (McMullen) | Extra sets: linear, quadratic, systems |
| Humongous Book of Algebra Problems (Kelley) | Act II boss book |
| Trigonometry Essentials (McMullen) | Act III drills — trig is the math of aim and of renderers |
| Stewart Precalculus: Mathematics for Calculus 8th + Solutions Manual | Act III theory spine |
| Humongous Book of SAT Math Problems | BENCHMARK ONLY — biweekly timed self-test, 25 mixed problems, log score |
| Calisthenics Playbook | Act 0 — runs from day one, forever |

Missing volume (get later, when Stewart feels comfortable): Calculus Made Easy (Thompson, free) -> OpenStax Calculus Vol 1 (free).

## The Acts

### Act 0 — Body (day 1, permanent)
15-20 min calisthenics on wake, before anything. Beginner circuit from the Playbook.
Why: most evidence-backed cognitive enhancer that exists; forces vertical; BDNF supports the relearning.

### Act I — Weeks 1-3: Basic Math & Pre-Algebra (Kelley)
- Front to back, by hand on tablet in Obsidian
- Protocol: attempt -> fail -> read solution -> re-attempt blind. Never copy
- Feels too easy? Good. Early wins ARE the reboot. Momentum is the whole game in month one

### Act II — Weeks 3-8: Algebra
- Open every session: 20-30 min McMullen drills (Master Essential Algebra Skills)
- Main read: Humongous Algebra Problems, same attempt-first protocol
- Algebra Essentials workbook for weak-spot extra sets

### Act III — Weeks 8-14: Trig + Precalc
- Drills: Trigonometry Essentials (McMullen)
- Theory: Stewart Precalculus, read for understanding
- Solutions manual checked ONLY after honest attempts

### Act IV — Week 14+: Calculus
- 3Blue1Brown "Essence of Calculus" first (visual intuition, ~3 hrs, free)
- Thompson "Calculus Made Easy" -> OpenStax Calculus Vol 1
- Then: Book of Proof (Hammack, free) — the true critical-thinking unlock. Can start in parallel once algebra is comfortable

## Benchmarking

Every 2 weeks: 25 mixed problems from the SAT book, timed, score logged in Obsidian.
Objective proof the arc works — for the days it doesn't feel like it.

## Expected Curve

- Pre-algebra -> solid algebra: weeks 4-6
- Trig: +3-4 weeks
- Comfortable with Calc 1 ideas: month 3-4

## Rules

1. 45-60 min minimum daily, pen on tablet, no exceptions
2. Attempt before peeking. Always
3. After every chapter: Feynman write-up (explain it with the book closed). The stall-point = tomorrow's first move
4. Consistency > binges. Myelin, not cramming
