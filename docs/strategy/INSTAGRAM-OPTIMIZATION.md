## 🎨 YOUR GRID (Content Strategy)

### Current Situation:
**You have:** Mix of 3D work, rap videos, other content  
**Problem:** Not immediately clear you do real estate/commercial work

### Solution: Create Highlights

**Instagram Highlights = Organized categories**

**Create these highlights:**

#### 1. "Real Estate" 
**Icon:** 🏠 or 🎥
**Content:** Put real estate videos here when you get them

#### 2. "3D Work"
**Icon:** 🎨 or 💎
**Content:** Your 3D projects

#### 3. "Commercial"
**Icon:** 🎬 or 💼
**Content:** Business videos, events

#### 4. "Music"
**Icon:** 🎵
**Content:** Rap videos, music content

#### 5. "Contact"
**Icon:** 📧 or 📱
**Content:** Photo with contact info, DM instructions

**Why this works:**
- Keeps all your content
- Organized by category
- Visitors can pick what interests them
- Shows you're versatile

---
