## 🎬 HOW TO SHOOT REAL ESTATE (If You Haven't Before)

**Watch these tutorials:**
- YouTube: "How to shoot real estate video"
- YouTube: "Real estate videography tips"
- YouTube: "Property tour filming"

**Key principles:**
1. **Show space** - Wide shots show room size
2. **Show details** - Close-ups of features
3. **Tell a story** - Entry → Living → Kitchen → Bedroom → Exterior
4. **Keep camera level** - Use gimbal
5. **Natural light** - If possible, shoot during day
6. **Speed is key** - Don't linger, keep it moving

**Practice shooting YOUR OWN space first.**  
**Get comfortable before showing up to client shoots.**
