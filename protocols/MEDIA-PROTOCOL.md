# Media Protocol — Intentional Consumption Only

The insight you already had, written down so it sticks:
"everything feels so stale or like slop" — that's not the content getting worse,
that's your brain recalibrating. Protect that signal. It's the reboot working.

## The Core Rule

**Nothing passive by default.** Every piece of media gets consumed on purpose or not at all.
The question before opening anything: *"What am I here for?"* No answer = close it.

## Games — Pure Intention Clause

- Games are the REWARD block (after body/math/build/job), never the default state
- Before queueing: name the intention out loud — "ranked grind for X", "aim work", "one game with the boys"
- When the intention is done, the session is done. No autopilot re-queue
- Comp practice (KovaaK's/Aimlabs/ranked) counts as training. Mindless scrolling-with-a-controller does not

## Twitch / YouTube / Shows — The Slop Filter

- **Default: off.** Not banned — demoted. They lost default status, that's all
- Allowed with intention: a specific VOD to study (comp gameplay review), a specific
  tutorial for a Light Years phase, 3B1B/Strang/Stat 110 (those are the curriculum, not slop)
- The test: *did I choose this, or did the algorithm choose it for me?*
- If you catch yourself 20 min into something you didn't choose: no guilt, just close. Noticing IS the win

## The Replacement Stack (what fills the freed hours)

1. **Evening reading slot** — Petzold / Feynman / Hamming (../tracks/LIBRARY-CATALOG.md)
2. **Music + thoughts: the Zen block.** Walking or lying down, music on, no screen.
   This is not wasted time — unfocused mode is when the brain consolidates everything
   the focused blocks loaded in. Schedule it like it's training, because it is
3. **Obsidian free-writing** — when the head is loud, dump it into a daily note.
   Sitting in your head with a pen is the whole point

## Boredom Clause

Boredom is withdrawal from the dopamine drip, and it passes in ~2 weeks of consistency.
Don't fill every silence. The silence is where the thoughts you're trying to meet again live.

## Weekly Audit (Sunday, with the job review)

- Hours: chosen media vs algorithm media (estimate honestly)
- One thing watched/read that was genuinely nourishing
- One slop pattern noticed -> one friction added (unfollow, log out, remove from home screen)
