## Gear Tier List

### S-Tier (Essentials)

| Gear | Why |
|---|---|
| Doorway pull-up bar | All pulling strength starts here |
| Adjustable parallettes | Deep dips, L-sits, handstand prep |

**Upgrade path:** Free-standing pull-up bar or power tower. More stable, clearance for muscle-ups.

### A-Tier (High Value)

| Gear | Why |
|---|---|
| Resistance bands | Front lever / planche progressions, assisted pulls |
| Yoga mat | Floor core, wrist protection |

**Upgrade path:** Gymnastic rings on the pull-up bar. Replaces a lot of gear, S-tier core and stability.

### B-Tier (Good, Not Required for Pure Cali)

| Gear | Why |
|---|---|
| Portable bench + dumbbells | Isolated strength, hybrid days |
| Ab sliders | Rollouts, compression |

**Upgrade path:** Ab wheel > sliders for stretch and core activation.

### Recovery Tier (Non-Negotiable for Aimmaxxing)

Massage gun, foam roller, peanut ball. Use after training and long aim sessions to keep mobility and avoid wrist/shoulder lockup.

## Starter Circuit (15-20 min, Act 0 default)

Use until the Calisthenics Playbook routine takes over.

1. **Warmup (2 min)** — arm circles, wrist prep on mat
2. **Pull-ups** — 3 sets, max clean reps (band assist if needed)
3. **Parallette dips** — 3 x 8-12 (knee height to start)
4. **Hollow hold** — 3 x 20-30 sec on mat
5. **Band pull-aparts** — 2 x 15 (shoulder health for mouse shoulder)
6. **Cooldown (3 min)** — foam roller upper back + peanut ball traps

## Posture Correction (Forward Head & Upper Crossed Syndrome)
*Sitting at screens causes tight chest/neck and weak upper back. Fix this daily:*

* **Chin Tucks (3 x 15)**: Draw your head straight back (making a double chin). Hold for 2 seconds. Strengthens deep neck flexors.
* **Prone Y-T-W-L raises (2 sets of 10 each)**: Lie face down on the mat. Raise arms in Y, T, W, and L positions, squeezing your shoulder blades together. Strengthens lower traps and rhomboids.
* **Doorway Pec Stretch (3 x 30 sec)**: Place forearms on doorway, step forward until you feel a deep stretch in your chest.

## Swimming Conditioning (UCS Antidote)
*Swimming is the ultimate posture restorer. It decompresses the spine and builds upper back endurance.*

* **Prep Drills (Dry Land)**:
  - Band pull-aparts (2 x 15) to open the shoulders.
  - Arm rotations to lubricate the shoulder sockets.
* **Pool Protocol (Freestyle & Backstroke)**:
  - Focus on *Backstroke* to force thoracic extension and open the chest.
  - Front crawl: focus on high-elbow recovery and full body rotation to avoid shoulder impingement.

## Cold Shock Protocol (CNS Reset)
*Use cold showers willingly to snap out of brain fog and reset the nervous system.*

* **Cold Shower (1-2 mins)**: Turn the dial to pure cold at the end of your shower (or straight on wake-up). Focus on controlling your breathing (slow, deep diaphragmatic breaths). 
* *Why:* Resets your nervous system, flushes out stress hormones, and spikes baseline dopamine.

## Training Rules

- Body block runs **before** math. Vertical before screens
- Recovery gear is part of training, not optional luxury
- Comp days: keep shoulders/wrists fresh. Short mobility > skipping recovery
- Rings / power tower are future upgrades, not blockers for starting tonight
