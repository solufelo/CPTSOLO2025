# Neuro Physical Calibration

Morning nervous system wake-up before screens. Pairs with FITNESS-SETUP.md (strength) and comp aim work (precision).

**S-Tier morning block (15-20 min):**

1. **Max effort sprints** — 4-6 x 20m or 15s all-out. Wake CNS, not cardio day
2. **Barefoot dead-weight juggle** — light dumbbells or sandbags, eyes sync with hands. 3-5 min
3. **Aimbeast target switching** — map your **120cm/360** sens through **Raw Accel natural curve** to effective **~28cm** feel in fast flicks. Document hit % and over/undershoot
