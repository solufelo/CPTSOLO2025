# CNS Grounding Routine — Anti-Anxiety Protocol

Use this routine to regulate your central nervous system (CNS), manage school debt, and maintain basic health/cleanliness when anxiety or laziness stalls you.

---

## 1. The CNS Calibration Block (Wake-up — 15 Mins)
*Calm the erratic breathing, stabilize the heart rate, and clear the anxiety baseline before touching any screen.*

* **Box Breathing (5 mins)**:
  1. Inhale slowly for 4 seconds.
  2. Hold your breath for 4 seconds.
  3. Exhale slowly for 4 seconds.
  4. Hold your breath for 4 seconds.
  *Why:* Directly stimulates the vagus nerve to down-regulate the sympathetic nervous system (fight-or-flight).
* **Hydration + Salt**: Drink a large glass of water with a pinch of sea salt. Calms physical anxiety and stabilizes blood pressure.
* **Calisthenics (10 mins)**: Beginner calisthenics circuit (FITNESS-SETUP.md). Force physical movement to burn off excess cortisol.

## 2. The Fuel Block (Morning — 15 Mins)
*Anxiety makes you skip meals, which drops your blood sugar and triggers panic loops. Force simple inputs.*

* **Zero-Decision High-Protein Meal**:
  - Easiest options: 3 scrambled eggs, a protein shake, or Greek yogurt with honey.
  - *Rule:* Eat this immediately after calisthenics. Do not think, just feed.

## 3. The 5-Minute Environmental Sweep (Morning — 5 Mins)
*Messy space = messy mind. Block room rot before it spreads.*

* Set a 5-minute timer.
* Pick up trash, throw dirty clothes in the hamper, and clear your desk surface.
* *Rule:* Stop exactly when the timer hits 0. You are building consistency, not trying to do deep spring cleaning.

## 4. School Debt Audit (Morning — 15 Mins)
*Fear of the number keeps you paralyzed. Demystify the debt.*

* **The Recon Block**: Spend 15 minutes checking your student portal.
* Write down the exact numbers:
  - Total balance owed.
  - Payment deadlines.
  - Payment portal links.
* *Rule:* Do not try to solve the debt today. Just write the numbers down. Objectifying the numbers removes their power over your subconscious.

***

## The "Delete" Shield (Dopamine Blocks)
*How to protect your environment from compulsions:*

* **Doomscrolling / Gooning**: The phone goes in the drawer in another room. Zero exceptions during work blocks.
* **Erratic physical ticks**: When you catch yourself breathing erratically or pacing compulsively, stop immediately, sit down, and do 3 rounds of box breathing.
