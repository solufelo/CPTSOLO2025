## 🚀 Why Local SEO?

**Local keywords are EASIER to rank for:**
- Less competition than national keywords
- Higher intent (people searching locally are ready to buy)
- Google prioritizes local businesses for "near me" searches
- Faster results (can rank in weeks instead of months)

### The Strategy:
Instead of trying to rank for "web developer" (impossible — millions of results), we target:
- "web developer Brampton" (much easier — thousands of results)
- "videographer Brampton" 
- "small business website Brampton"

## 🗺️ LocalBusiness Schema Markup

All local blog posts include structured data:

```json
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "LocalBusiness",
  "name": "CaptainSolo - [Service] Brampton",
  "address": {
    "@type": "PostalAddress",
    "addressLocality": "Brampton",
    "addressRegion": "ON",
    "addressCountry": "CA"
  },
  "geo": {
    "@type": "GeoCoordinates",
    "latitude": "43.7315",
    "longitude": "-79.7624"
  },
  "areaServed": ["Brampton", "Mississauga", "Toronto", "GTA", "Ontario"],
  "serviceType": [...]
}
```

**This helps with:**
- Google Maps listings
- "Near me" searches
- Local 3-Pack results
- Mobile voice searches
