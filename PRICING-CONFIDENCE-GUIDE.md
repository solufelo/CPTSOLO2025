## 🛡️ Handling Objections (Without Freaking Out)

### Objection 1: "That's too expensive"

**Response:**
```
"I understand budget is important. Let me break down the value:

A professional website normally costs $2,000-5,000 from an agency.
Fiverr might be $200, but you get a template with no support.

At $400, you get:
- Custom design (not a template)
- Personal service (I'm local and accountable)
- Training (I'll teach you how to manage it)
- Ongoing support (not ghosting after delivery)

I'm only doing this rate for my first 3 portfolio projects.
If budget doesn't work, I completely understand. 
Let me know if that changes."
```

**Key:** Be willing to walk away. Don't negotiate down.

---

### Objection 2: "What if I don't like it?"

**Response:**
```
"I guarantee you'll love it. Here's how it works:

1. We chat about what you want (15 mins)
2. I build it (2 weeks)
3. You review it and request changes (free revisions)
4. You approve it
5. You pay

If you're not happy after 2 rounds of revisions, 
I'll refund you 100%. Fair?"
```
